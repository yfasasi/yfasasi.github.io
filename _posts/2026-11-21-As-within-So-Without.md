**"As Above, so below; As Within, so without"**
---
The human mind is one of the most powerful resources. Every idea, whether good or bad, has been one that was conceived in the mind first. As powerful as our mind is, it can also be our greatest undoing. For whatever occupies your mind the most turns reflect in your daily life. That is why you must make sure to take care of your mind as you take care of your body. 

---

**What does this phrase really mean?**

When stripped bare, the phrase means your outer life, what you project on the outside is a reflection of what is happening within. It does'nt mean you are in control of every single thing that happen to you, as some things are out of control no matter how hard you try. But it does mean you can sometimes shape how you interpret the world by projecting a positive outlook. If you pre-occupy your mind with how there is not a single good person anymore in the world, even when you come across a person who means well, they are not going to be of any benefit to you. If you constantly worry about how everyone in the world is out to get you, you will wear yourself out unnecessarily. 
> **Key Thing:** You do not see the world as it is, you see the world as *you* are.

---
**Three Ways Your Inner State Mirror Your Outer Life**

**1. Your Focus Filter Your Reality**

Ever wonder why our brain naturally optimizes for the information that confirms our bias. We usually lean toward details that solidify our existing beliefs. Psychologists call this *The confirmation bias.* When you have already position your brain to believe that everyone is bad, your brain will highlight every slight, expands every little inconveniences far beyond what they really are, and you will mostly distrust everyone. This is how your thought matches a version of reality it was already programmed to believe.

**2. Your Environment reflect your mental clutter**

Take a look at your desk, your workspace, your kitchen. Often, prolonged internal chaos manifest as external disorderliness. When you see yourself taking disinterest in the activities that you once cherished, it is often a manifestation of what is going on deep inside.

**3. How You Treat Yourself Sets the Standard for Others**

People notice when you stop setting healthy boundaries, when you have given up on yourself. How people treat you, often times, is a reflection of how you carry yourself. And some times, a reflection of how you treat others too. If you nurture the idea that everyone is bad, you start to treat them badly.  

---

**How to Apply this Principle Today**

- Take a mental inventory of your thoughts: Notice the recurring story you tell yourself when things go wrong. The harsh judgements you project onto others   without knowing the full story. Replace those with curiosity and open mindset. When you found yourself slipping, take a step back and audit your thought.
- Change small external habit one at a time: Going for a walk might help you clear your mind. Cleaning your rooms and organizing your study space might       help you clarify your thought. The key is in taking active steps to shape your overall outlook on life.

---

**Final Thought**

Your external world is actively giving you feedback. Tend to your inner mindset. Take a stock of what constantly occupies your mind and redirect them in a positive way. Remember, just because you see it that way doesnt mean it is the reality.
