## About Me

Hi, I'm **Yussuf Fasasi**, a PhD student in Computational Materials Science. My research focuses on using atomistic simulations, high-throughput workflows, and DFT to understand and design materials.

### What You'll Find Here
* **Tutorials & Code:** Step-by-step guides, new tools in Materials Sciences, Jupyter notebooks, and workflows for **ASE**, **pymatgen**, DFT post-processing, life as I navigate grad school. As I sharpen my skills in ML, machine learning workflows will be incorporated too.
* **Research & Publications:** Updates on my ongoing projects, papers, and conference presentations.
* **Blog & Life:** Notes from conferences, lessons as I navigate life, and photography outside the lab.

Feel free to explore the **Tutorials** and **Blog Posts** tabs above, or reach out via email or GitHub if you'd like to collaborate!
