## About Me

Hi, I'm **Yussuf**, working in the area of Computational Materials Science. My work focuses on using atomistic simulations, high-throughput workflows, and DFT to understand and design materials.

* **Research & Publications:** Updates on my ongoing projects, papers, and conference presentations.
* **Blog & Life:** Notes from conferences, excerpts from books I read, lessons as I navigate life, and nature photography .
